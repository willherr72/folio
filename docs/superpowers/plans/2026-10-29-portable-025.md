2. Extraction or cwd dependency: full archive verification and outside-checkout runs.
3. Editing regression hidden by save success: independent full-text/order, glyph,
   raster, font and nonempty structure checks; exact Undo/Redo and source ownership.
4. Reuse hiding stale discovery or failures: per-source/page call counts, retried
   refusal-bearing providers and changed-source hit targets in packaged UI.
5. Evidence inflation or private material in package: terminal counts, preserved
   limitations/failures, sanitized quiz evidence, package inventory and old integrity.
